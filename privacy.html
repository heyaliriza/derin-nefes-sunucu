Deep Breath (Derin Nefes) Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

In short: Deep Breath needs no account and never asks for your real name, email, location or contacts; it contains no ads, analytics or tracking. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game only goes online for two things: "Play Online", to carry the match to your friend's phone (nothing of it is stored), and the leaderboard, to send your score (a random player ID, the nickname you choose, and the score are stored). You can turn score sending off on the Leaderboard screen and delete the scores you sent there with one tap.

What this policy covers

This page covers the Android game "Derin Nefes" / "Deep Breath" (package name com.derinnefes.oyun) and the browser version of the game. "We" means the game's developer.

Information that stays on your phone

Levels, stars, pearls, wardrobe choices, statistics and settings are kept only in the game's save file on your device. It is never sent to us and is excluded from Google backups. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Play Online

When you use this feature, the game opens an encrypted connection (WSS/TLS) to our room server, which relays the match between the two players. The server runs on Render (render.com). Only the following is sent:

  • the 4-letter room code,
  • a random connection ID created anew each time the game starts (not tied to you or your device),
  • your character's look (outfit, hat, pet, colour),
  • match messages: an encrypted digest of your move, the move revealed at the end of the round, your bet, preset emotes (bubble talk) and rematch requests.

This information is held only in the server's memory, only while you are connected, so it can be passed instantly to the other player in the same room; it is deleted when you leave. It is never written to disk, a database or log files. There is no free-text chat. As with any internet connection, the hosting infrastructure technically processes your IP address to deliver the connection; we do not store or use it.

Leaderboard

When you finish a run in Speedrun, Souls or Endless mode, the game sends your score to the leaderboard. Only the following is sent:

  • Player ID: a random 24-digit number created on your phone the first time you open the game. It is not tied to your device, account, advertising ID or to you; it is only used to recognise your own row.
  • Nickname: either a name you type yourself (3-16 characters) or one the game builds from preset words with the dice button (e.g. "Jolly Whale 6"). You can change it at any time. Everyone can see it on the board; do not use your real name, surname or contact details.
  • Score: the board's name (Speedrun, Souls, Endless), your best value (a time or a count) and when it was last updated.

This information is stored on the server until you delete it and is shown publicly on the board with your nickname. Other players' IDs are never sent to anyone. To protect the server from abuse, the requesting IP address is counted in memory for 10 minutes only and is never written to disk.

Name rules and reporting: Names that contain insults, swearing, hate speech, sexual content, impersonation or personal information are not allowed. Typed names pass through a bad-word filter. If you see an offensive name on the board, tap the flag next to it to report it; the name is hidden on your phone right away. Once two different players report a name, it is hidden for everyone and that player shows up with a dice-generated name. We review reports too, and a player who breaks the rules can lose the ability to type a name. When you report a name, the reported name, your player ID and the date are stored (so the same person can't report a name more than once); "Delete My Scores" deletes these records too.

Turning it off and deleting: the "Send My Scores" switch on the Leaderboard screen stops sending. The "Delete My Scores" button immediately deletes your scores from every board on the server. If you uninstall the game without doing this, a new install creates a new ID, so your old scores can no longer be linked to you; in that case you can ask us to delete them by writing to us.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information for advertising or marketing. The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking libraries. The server runs on Render (render.com); leaderboard scores may be kept in a database service (e.g. Neon, neon.tech). These services only store the information on our behalf.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only for Play Online and the leaderboard.
  • Vibration: light haptic feedback on taps; can be turned off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for players of all ages and never asks anyone, including children, for their name, email or contact details. Online play only happens between friends who share a room code. The leaderboard only shows nicknames; names are filtered, can be reported and get hidden. There is no chat or messaging.

Your rights

We do not store match messages. You can delete the scores and nickname you sent to the leaderboard at any time with the "Delete My Scores" button in the game; you can also write to us to have them deleted or to learn which scores are kept. You can always delete the save file on your phone by uninstalling the game or clearing its app data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with the date above.
